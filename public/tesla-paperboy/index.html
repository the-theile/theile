How to Play

📦Throw — Tap the upper half of the screen to toss a package toward that spot
🏠Deliver — Hit the glowing porches for points. Watch for pools, Ring cameras & porch pirates
⚡FSD — After several good deliveries, Autopilot engages so you can focus on throwing
🚪Gates — Sometimes you’ll need a gate code. Guess wrong and the HOA gets involved
